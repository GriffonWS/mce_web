FAQ

Questions planters ask

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Attendance, worker changes, fertilizer applications and stock purchases can be recorded offline. They sync automatically when your phone is back online.

How are union wages calculated?

From the Cardamom Growers Association circular in force for each day (Basic + DA), plus weightage for years of service and ₹3.25 allowances for spraying and shade work. You can edit the rates if your area differs.

Is my estate data safe?

Your data travels over encrypted connections and only your account can see your estate. We never sell your data or show ads. Read our Privacy Policy.

What happens after the free trial?

You move to the Free plan automatically — nothing is charged. Upgrade to Pro anytime for unlimited workers, AI scans and PDF reports.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep Pro until the end of the paid year.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, anytime from Profile → Delete account. See how account deletion works.
